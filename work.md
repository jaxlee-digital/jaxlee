  <p class="lede">Full-stack web developer and team lead with over a decade of production Ruby on Rails experience. I design, build, and maintain large web applications that stay readable, testable, and useful years after launch.</p>

  <p>I work best on small, remote teams that take UX, testing, and clean architecture seriously. Comfortable owning a feature end to end: scoping with stakeholders, writing the code, reviewing the PRs, shipping the release, and supporting it in production. Equally comfortable stepping into a legacy codebase, finding what's load-bearing, and improving it without breaking it.</p>

  <p>As a consultant I've focused particularly on early-stage and women-owned startups. I help founders define an MVP that's actually shippable, shape a product roadmap that survives contact with real users, and make the technical decisions that are expensive to revisit later. When the code is already there, I dig in, identify what's slowing the team down, and deliver fixes that balance speed, stability, and long-term maintainability.</p>
