Out of Goodland Boat Park with Adam. Ran the mangrove islands,
anchored off a channel looking for bait. Adam caught the biggest
sail catfish either of us had seen. Beached on a remote island,
walked the shell line.

Lunch at the Little Bar. Dockside, old Florida, hasn't changed
and doesn't need to.
