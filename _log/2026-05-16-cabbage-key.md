A day trip out to Cabbage Key with Adam. Hundred-acre island in
Pine Island Sound, no underwater cable for power or phone, reachable
only by boat. The Rinehart family bought the place in 1936 for two
thousand dollars and built their winter estate on top of an old
Calusa shell mound. The main house has been a public inn since 1944.
Same family (the Wells) has run it since 1976. National Register of
Historic Places in 2016.

The Rinehart name carries some weight: Alan's mother, Mary Roberts
Rinehart, was one of the most successful fiction writers of her era.
Her play *The Bat* is said to have been Bob Kane's inspiration for
Batman.

The royal poincianas were in full bloom. Climbed the 1930s water
tower (one of the highest points in all of southwest Florida, which
says more about the geography than the tower) for the view over the
mangroves and the cut. Worth the trip.
