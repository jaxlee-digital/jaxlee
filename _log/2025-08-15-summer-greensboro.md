Adam picked up a union job in North Carolina last summer, so the
three of us packed up and lived in Greensboro for a few months.
Florida swapped for piedmont. A different rhythm.

While Adam worked through the week, Jaxon and I made the North
Carolina Zoo our thing. The zoo is in Asheboro, about thirty minutes
south of town. We went most weekends. He climbed the bronze elephant
and rhino out front every visit, watched the giraffes get fed,
worked his way through the insect playground. By the end he had
opinions about which path to take first.

On the weekends Adam was off, we drove out for hikes in the
Sauratown range and the foothills west of town. Hanging Rock and the
Moore's Knob fire tower. Long views from old stone summits, the kind
of country you forget exists when you live in Florida.

One stretch we took a trip up to Boone and stayed in a cabin in the
mountains. Hot tub on the deck looking out over the Blue Ridge,
creek running through the property. Neka and Georgia came up with
us and lived in the water. A luna moth on the porch one morning. Jaxon
played Yahtzee with us at night and spent the days in the water.

Three months in the piedmont, then back home. Worth the trip.
