Twenty years. We've been coming back to Green Turtle Cay for twenty years.

Houseboats, houses, different configurations of the K family at different stages of life — but always this place. Something about it just sticks.

This year we packed up the whole crew: siblings, significant others, the next generation already in full stride. A big house, three golf carts, a boat, and that water. That ridiculous, impossible turquoise water that somehow still catches you off guard every time.

Jaxon wore a Spider-Man life vest and mirrored sunglasses and had zero concerns. Correct behavior.

We hit the ferry dock in matching shirts (Green Turtle Cay 2026, K Family — yes, we do the shirts), found our way to Big O's Swimming Pigs, waded into the flats until we couldn't anymore, and did what we always do here — nothing and everything all at once.

### The year before

And then there's this version of Jaxon — a little smaller, a little more awestruck, but the Spider-Man vest was already a thing.

A stingray encounter in the shallows. Jaxon's dollar pinned to the wall at the bar (you can read it right there: *Jaxon Mangham, 8/2026*). Golf cart rides through the island. A pool tower. The fishing rods. That kid staring out at the open ocean like he owned it.

He's been coming here his whole life. One day he'll realize what that means.
