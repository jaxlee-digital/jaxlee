I have been using the [Dygma Defy](https://dygma.com/products/dygma-defy) for a while now and wanted to share my thoughts.

## Wireless: Don't Bother

If you are looking for a wireless keyboard, look elsewhere — full stop. The battery, without underglow, lasts about three days. Not a total deal breaker on its own, but the charging process is what kills it. You have to use the included dongle to charge both halves simultaneously while using them. That means pulling the dongle from the bottom of the keyboard, grabbing all the cords, and plugging both boards in. When you want to go back to wireless, you unplug the dongle, plug it back in, and restart the keyboard. Too much friction. I now use it wired exclusively.

## The Keyboard Itself: Great

Wireless gripes aside, this is a genuinely great ergonomic keyboard. Once your brain reconciles with the split layout, key locations transition seamlessly. The thumb clusters take some adjustment but quickly become second nature.

## Key Layout

I use Bazecor to manage my key mappings. I will add my current layout to GitHub soon.
