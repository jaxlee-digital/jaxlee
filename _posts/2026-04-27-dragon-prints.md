Jaxon, who is four, has opinions. He wanted dragons. He wanted them in
specific colors. He wanted three of them.

So far there are two. The first one came off the bed in pink. The
second in white. The third color is still being negotiated.

These aren't my designs. I printed them on the Bambu Lab A1 mini from
files Jaxon picked out. Articulated flexi prints, the kind that come
off the build plate already moving. The novelty doesn't wear off, at
least not at four.
